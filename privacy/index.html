Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Get Payments, Inc., doing business as MyAiValet ("MyAiValet," "we," "our," or "us"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our AI concierge services, and describes your choices.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity Data: first name, last name, and business name.
  • Contact Data: email address and mobile or telephone numbers.
  • Communications Data: messages you send us, and your text-message and call consent and opt-out preferences.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, and usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies.

3. How We Use Phone Numbers

If you provide your phone number and opt in, we collect and use it to send you transactional messages (such as replies to your inquiries, appointment and service updates, and account notifications) and promotional messages (such as occasional offers and product updates). Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

4. SMS Consent & Mobile Information

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the Text Message services.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We never sell or rent your mobile number or SMS consent.

5. AI-Assisted Calls

If you separately consent on our contact form, we may call you at the number provided, including with AI-assisted or artificial or prerecorded voice calls, about your inquiry and related services. We record the date, time, and source of that consent. Call consent is optional, is not a condition of purchase, and is never shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. You can revoke it at any time by telling us during a call, replying STOP to a text, or emailing laura@getondeets.ai. Calls may be handled or transcribed by our AI concierge and service providers solely to respond to you.

6. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide our services.
  • To send transactional and, with your consent, promotional communications.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf under confidentiality obligations (such as hosting, messaging aggregators, and text and voice service providers), when required by law, or in connection with a merger or acquisition. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are shared only as described in Section 4.

8. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored with reputable cloud service providers in the United States. We protect it with encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls limited to authorized personnel, and other reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

9. Your Rights & Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing laura@getondeets.ai. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law, including California privacy law (CCPA/CPRA).

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version with a new "Last Updated" date.
